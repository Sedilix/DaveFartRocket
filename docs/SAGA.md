# DAVE IS FINE, PART 2: THE HUSHENING

*A saga about a man, a bean, and the thirty-nine ways a universe can be politely inconvenienced.*

**Logline.** Dave destroyed Earth with a fart, and he is fine. Rescued by the Quell, a species so quiet they apologise to furniture, he learns every fart he has ever done was the first of a doubling curse from a single brunch bean. Their Hush Field can swallow the blast but not the fart. So begins a journey through five voxel worlds to find the one thing that can fix him: a fart that makes no sound at all.

## Tone rules

1. **Deadpan understatement is the engine.** The narrator (the same dry voice from the opening: "Meet Dave. He's having a perfectly normal Tuesday.") never raises its voice, however large the catastrophe. The bigger the disaster, the smaller the sentence.
2. **Dave is fine.** He is always, miraculously, fine. Everything around him is not. Dave never gets a nasty line; he is a sincere, slightly oblivious good guy. The comedy comes from the world reacting to him.
3. **Nobody says the word.** Everyone calls it something else: *an event*, *the incident*, *the noise*, *a disturbance in the field*. The Quell never say it, ever. The Nomads of Nom say it constantly, with reverence.
4. **Escalate by changing the scale of the damage, not the joke.** Houses, cities, a moon, a star. The sentence stays the same.
5. **Warm underneath.** Pim and Dave's friendship is the real story. The last act has to land.

## The cause

On the Tuesday of the first game, Dave had brunch at the outdoor café. The special was a **Doubling Bean**, a banned agricultural pest from the far side of the galaxy. It doesn't digest. It *grows*, and every fart it produces is exactly twice as strong as the last. Fart #1 was 54 dB. By #25 it was 16,777,216 times stronger and Earth was gone.

The Quell banned the bean 4,000 years ago after *The Incident at Kepler* (a single burp that rearranged a small moon). They say that name very quietly.

## The Hush Field

A bubble of calm worn on the Quell's wrists, strapped to Dave. It **absorbs the shockwave of each fart**: no more blown-away buildings, no more screaming pedestrians. It cannot stop the fart itself. Dave still farts. The field still gets hit.

- **Gameplay:** the *Hush meter*. Each fart drains it by an amount that doubles, same as everything else. Pim can recharge it between farts by *humming* (a short rhythm minigame), but never fully.
- The field also **mutes** the sound for everyone but Dave and the player. The world doesn't hear it. The audience does. That is the joke.

## The structure

### Prologue: *Tuesday* (the game as it is)
Farts #1 to #25. The street, the pigeons, the dog (who is concerned), the rocket ascent, the moon, the Earth. The Earth explodes. A very small, quiet sentence appears: *"Dave is fine."*

### Act I: *Rescued, Loudly*
Dave drifts in the debris. A tiny silent ship, the *Whisper*, glides up. First contact is a disaster of manners: the Quell try a gentle greeting, Dave says "Hi! Sorry about Earth," and does fart #26 in the airlock. The Hush Field activates for the first time, just barely. The captain faints. Pim is volunteered, in a whisper, against his will.
**Briefing** (in a hush-proof room with a lot of cushions): the bean, the doubling, and the fact that the Quell have never once seen a cure.
**Gameplay:** a short tutorial in the ship (voxel interior). Learn to walk, to hold the Hush meter, and not to press RELEASE near the fragile things (which is everything).

### Act II: *The Moon of Nom* (jungle voxel planet)
The bean grew wild here. The Nomads treat farts as sacred. Dave is carried through the village on a litter. He is asked to open the **Festival of the Great Gust**. He tries to decline. Nobody lets him.
**Beats:** a chief who keeps adopting Dave's casualties as "blessings"; the Nomads rebuild a collapsed bridge using Dave's fart as a hydraulic jack; Pim, hiding in a hat, translates badly on purpose.
**Gameplay:** build and break blocks. Use fart-power to blast a path through the jungle (fart bridge puzzles). A first clue: the bean's recipe is carved on a ritual drum.

### Act III: *Frostbite Hotel* (ice voxel planet)
A grand empty hotel on a frozen moon, run by an unusually nervous concierge. It is Chef Glorb, who sold the bean. He confesses over a cold, tragic tasting menu. He never meant any of it. "It was meant to be the best brunch in the galaxy." He knows one thing: the **Whisper Bean**, the cure, grows only at the Source.
**Beats:** a ghost-hunting subplot in which the only ghost is a very embarrassed lodger; an avalanche triggered by a single sneeze; Dave attempts to hold a fart in an elevator.
**Gameplay:** explore the hotel as a voxel building; escape the avalanche using a fart-jump chain.

### Act IV: *The Elder's Offer* (desert voxel planet)
Elder Sibilance meets Dave under a sky full of quiet stars and offers a deal: *"Be sealed in a silent vault for ten thousand years. The universe is spared. You will not feel a thing."* It is the reasonable answer, and the narrator knows it. Pim refuses. A chase across the dunes: the vault-ship pursues, the Hush meter drains, and by now the numbers are planetary (**fart #35 = 17 billion times stronger than the first**).
**Beats:** the first time Dave understands what he really is to them; Pim's big speech, delivered at a whisper to a dune that doesn't care; Captain Murmur finally stays conscious for a whole scene.
**Gameplay:** a long run across the desert with sandstorms (voxel terrain, digging cover), the Hush meter as a ticking resource.

### Act V: *The Bean Tree at the End of the Universe*
A dead star. A single enormous tree. A single bean. The cure isn't a thing you eat. It is a **silent fart**, the only fart the doubling cannot grow from. Anyone can do it, if they can hold the moment.
**The final mechanic:** the tension ring, which the player has been racing since the first level, runs **backwards**. You are not trying to press RELEASE. You are trying to wait. Breathe. Let the ring fill, hold your nerve, and release at exactly the right moment. A lot is riding on it. The whole saga rides on one tiny, silent, absolutely correct fart.
The doubling resets to **×1**. Dave's next fart, a small, normal, almost polite noise, is greeted by a standing ovation from the entire Quell fleet.

### Epilogue: *Brunch*
Dave asks the Quell to help him rebuild Earth. They agree, in a whisper. The **voxel sandbox is Earth, rebuilt block by block** by the player. The closing line is the narrator's: *"Dave is fine. Dave would like the toast."* A tiny bean sprouts on a new planet in the last shot.

## Running gags

- **The casualty counter.** A tiny HUD line that keeps rising and gets gently renamed: *Houses Inconvenienced → Cities Inconvenienced → Moons Inconvenienced → Stars Reassigned.*
- **"Dave is fine."** Every act ends with it. In Act IV it is said by someone who really needs it to be true.
- **Names for the thing:** a running list of euphemisms the Quell invent (*"an unscheduled exhale"*, *"a vocal hardship"*, *"the weather"*).
- **The narrator** starts using longer and longer words for the same event, then goes silent once, and the silence is worse.
- **Pigeons.** They survive everything. A pigeon is somehow present in the last shot.
- **The dog.** The dog from the street is rescued in Act I. It never explains how it got there.

## How it plugs into the game

- **Street/rocket game** stays as the Prologue.
- **Voxel mode** hosts Acts II to V as procedurally generated planets with biome presets, and the Epilogue as a sandbox where you rebuild Earth.
- **Reusable systems:** NPC reactions (the Nomads and the Quell react with the same tier system), the narrator subtitle line, the tension ring (inverted in the finale), onomatopoeia popups, and the leashed-dog gag.
